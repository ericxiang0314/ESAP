About this app

Discover Steam games that match your style

This app helps you browse a large Steam game catalog, filter games by genre, language, and tags, and view detailed information for each title.

You can also create saved profiles with your favorite preferences, and the app will recommend games that fit those choices. It is designed to make exploring new games feel simple, personal, and easy to use.
